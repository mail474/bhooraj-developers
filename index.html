FAQ

Common questions

What is the price of plots at Shree Radha Enclave?

Plots start from ₹8,450 per gaj (sq. yd). For example, an 88.88 gaj plot comes to about ₹7.51 lakh at the starting rate. The final price depends on the plot; registry and other charges are extra. Ask us for the current price list.

What plot sizes are available?

There are 30 residential plots from 88.88 to 174.94 gaj, plus 11 commercial shops. Use the plot finder above to see each plot.

Where exactly is the project?

At Village Boraj, near Boraj Garh, Tehsil Mozmabad, off Ajmer Road, Jaipur. Plus code VFF3+C85. Tap "Get directions" in the Location section.

Which amenities are included?

Solar street lights, tar roads (60 ft main and 30 ft internal), a security guard, water supply, electricity, a gated entrance, a park, a temple and commercial shops.

Can I visit the site before booking?

Yes. Choose "Site visit" in the enquiry form with a preferred date, and our team will arrange it.

Is the colony approved?

Shree Radha Enclave is a society-approved colony. Ask us to see the approval documents for your chosen plot.
