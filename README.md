# Bhooraj Developers — website

One file, `index.html`. No build step, no server. It runs on any free static host.

## Go live free in about 2 minutes (Netlify Drop)
1. Open https://app.netlify.com/drop and sign up free (Google login works).
2. Drag this whole folder onto the page.
3. You get a live link like `something.netlify.app`. In **Site settings → Change site name**, rename it to e.g. `bhoorajdevelopers.netlify.app`.

Other free options: **GitHub Pages** (upload `index.html` to a public repo → Settings → Pages → deploy from `main`) or **Cloudflare Pages** (Workers & Pages → Create → Upload assets).

## Turn on the enquiry form (one-time)
The form emails enquiries to **Bhurajdevelopers@gmail.com** through FormSubmit.co, which is free with no account.
1. After the site is live, submit one test enquiry yourself.
2. FormSubmit sends an **activation email** to Bhurajdevelopers@gmail.com. Check Spam too, and click **Activate**.
3. From then on, every enquiry arrives in that inbox as a neat table, with the visitor's email as the reply-to address.

If the form can't send for any reason, visitors see the enquiry text and a button to email it to you directly. No enquiry is lost silently.

## Editing
- Email and map pin: the `SITE` block near the bottom of `index.html`.
- Text: search for the sentence in `index.html` and change it.
- Phone or WhatsApp number, prices, plot sizes, approvals and real photos are not on the site yet. Add them once they're confirmed.
